<idea>
  **Design Movement**: **Neo-Brutalist Noir**
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Raw Authenticity**: Unapologetic use of bold borders, high contrast, and raw materials (paper textures, ink blots).
  2. **Typewriter Aesthetic**: Embracing the requested typewriter font as a core structural element, not just decoration.
  3. **Tactile Interactivity**: Buttons feel like physical keys, cards feel like manila folders.
  4. **High-Stakes Drama**: The legal world is dramatic; the design should reflect the weight of decisions.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Intent**: To evoke the feeling of old legal documents, detective noir, and urgent stamps.
  - **Palette**:
    - Backgrounds: Warm Paper (#F5F0E6), Deep Charcoal (#1A1A1A) for dark mode.
    - Accents: Alert Red (#FF4444) for "Objection!", Highlighter Yellow (#FFF500) for emphasis, Ink Black (#000000).
    - *Note*: Strictly avoiding blues and purples as requested.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Asymmetric Collage**: Elements are slightly rotated or offset, mimicking a messy desk of a busy lawyer.
  - **Stacked Cards**: Information is presented in overlapping files and papers.

  **Signature Elements**:
  1. **Paper Textures**: Subtle grain and fold lines on containers.
  2. **Monospaced Typography**: Courier Prime or similar for all text, with varying weights.
  3. **Stamp Motifs**: "APPROVED", "DENIED", "CASE CLOSED" visual stamps for feedback.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Mechanical Clicks**: Hover states simulate the depression of a typewriter key.
  - **Stamping Actions**: Completing a task feels like stamping a document.

  **Animation**:
  - **Stop-Motion Feel**: Jerky, frame-by-frame transitions for a retro vibe.
  - **Typewriter Effect**: Text appears character by character.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: **Courier Prime Bold** (Scale: 2.5rem+) - Heavy, authoritative.
  - **Body**: **Courier Prime Regular** (Scale: 1rem) - Legible, document-style.
  - **Accents**: **Special Elite** - Gritty, worn typewriter look for stamps and emphasis.
</idea>

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: **Retro-Futurist Terminal**
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Digital Justice**: A gamified interface that looks like a hacker's terminal but accessible to the masses.
  2. **Data Transparency**: Information is presented in clear, grid-less blocks of data.
  3. **Neon Accents**: Using non-blue/purple neons to highlight critical paths.
  4. **Accessibility First**: High contrast is built into the core aesthetic, not just an add-on.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Intent**: To make law feel like a solvable code or system.
  - **Palette**:
    - Backgrounds: Deep Forest Green (#0A1F0A) for dark mode, Pale Mint (#E0F2E9) for light mode.
    - Accents: Amber (#FFBF00) for warnings, Matrix Green (#00FF41) for success, Hot Pink (#FF00CC) for critical alerts.
    - *Note*: Avoiding standard blues/purples; using green/amber/pink spectrum.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Split-Screen Command Center**: Navigation on the left, active task on the right, always visible status bars.
  - **Modular Blocks**: Content lives in distinct, bordered containers.

  **Signature Elements**:
  1. **Scanlines**: Subtle CRT effects (optional toggle).
  2. **Blinking Cursors**: Prompts user attention.
  3. **ASCII Art**: Used for badges and icons.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Instant Feedback**: Interactions are snappy and immediate.
  - **Keyboard Centric**: Heavy focus on keyboard navigation visual cues.

  **Animation**:
  - **Glitch Effects**: Subtle glitches on transition.
  - **Slide-in Panels**: UI elements slide in from off-screen like terminal windows.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: **VT323** - Pixelated, large.
  - **Body**: **Fira Code** or **Roboto Mono** - Clean, readable monospaced.
  - **Hierarchy**: Differentiated by color and background highlights rather than just size.
</idea>

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: **Bauhaus Constructivism**
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Form Follows Function**: Every element has a clear purpose; no decoration for decoration's sake.
  2. **Geometric Clarity**: Use of circles, triangles, and squares to represent legal concepts.
  3. **Bold Typography**: Typewriter font treated as a graphic element.
  4. **Playful Structure**: Law is serious, but learning it can be playful through geometry.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Intent**: To make the complex simple and approachable.
  - **Palette**:
    - Backgrounds: Off-White (#F0F0F0), Matte Black (#202020).
    - Accents: Primary Red (#E63946), Mustard Yellow (#E9C46A), Burnt Orange (#F4A261).
    - *Note*: Warm, earthy, and primary tones. No blues/purples.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Grid-Breaking Geometry**: Elements align to a grid but break it with diagonal lines or circular overlays.
  - **Floating Shapes**: Background elements that shift with parallax.

  **Signature Elements**:
  1. **Geometric Badges**: Badges are simple combinations of shapes.
  2. **Diagonal Dividers**: Sections separated by angled lines.
  3. **Oversized Type**: Headlines are massive and structural.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Physics-Based**: Elements have weight and bounce.
  - **Shape Morphing**: Buttons change shape on hover.

  **Animation**:
  - **Geometric Transitions**: Screens wipe with circular or triangular masks.
  - **Bouncy Motion**: Elements spring into place.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: **Space Mono Bold** - Geometric, monospaced.
  - **Body**: **Space Mono Regular** - Clean, readable.
  - **Pairing**: Occasional use of a geometric sans-serif for very small text if mono is too wide.
</idea>
